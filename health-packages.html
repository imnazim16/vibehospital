Patient Guidelines

How to Prepare for Your Visit

Simple instructions to ensure 100% accurate and reliable laboratory readings.

1
10-12 Hrs Fasting

Do not consume food, tea, coffee, or milk after 9 PM the previous night. Water is permitted.

2
Morning Medications

Take routine hypertension medicines with a sip of water. Hold morning diabetic medications until sample is collected.

3
Past Medical Records

Carry past prescriptions, previous scan reports, discharge summaries, and current eyeglasses.

4
Comfortable Attire

Wear loose two-piece clothing and sport shoes if scheduled for a treadmill stress test (TMT).
