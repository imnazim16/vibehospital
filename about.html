Our Origin & Heritage

25 Years of Clinical Mastery & Compassion

From our founding in 2001 to becoming one of North India’s most trusted multi-organ care destinations.

VibeMed Hospital was founded with a singular conviction: that every patient deserves access to internationally benchmarked clinical expertise delivered with uncompromised human warmth. Today, our campus houses 30+ specialty institutes, 500+ senior clinicians, and over 1,200 trained nursing and allied professionals.

We combine robotic surgical suites, digital critical care units, advanced genomic pathology, and comprehensive organ transplant facilities under one integrated campus.
